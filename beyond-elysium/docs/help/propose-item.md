# Propose an Item

Your character made a thing, found a thing, or holds a thing. This is how you ask a Storyteller to make it real.

## Who can use this

Any player, for a character they own. A Storyteller doesn't need it - they add items straight to the catalog from [Items & Locations](world-objects.md).

## How to get there

My Chronicle → pick your character → **Propose an Item** tab.

A proposal belongs to a character, so you have to pick one first. If the tab tells you to choose a character, go to the Characters tab, open the one you mean, then come back.

## What happens

1. You fill in what the thing is, its name, a description, and whatever details its kind asks for.
2. It goes into the **Approval Queue** - the same list your Storytellers already work through for trait changes. It is not a separate list somebody has to remember to check.
3. A Storyteller approves or rejects it.

**If it's approved**, two things happen together: the item is added to your chronicle's catalog, and it is connected to your character. You asked for your character to have it, so approving gives you both.

**If it's rejected**, nothing is written anywhere. Your Storyteller can leave a note saying why - often that the chronicle already has something like it.

## What it costs

Nothing. An item is not an experience purchase. If your chronicle wants a particular item to cost something, that's a separate change against your sheet, handled the usual way.

## Kinds of thing

- **Item** - a weapon, a relic, a piece of equipment.
- **Location** - a haven, a business, a place that matters.
- **Rote** - for Mage characters, a worked magical effect.

Pick the kind first: the fields underneath change to match it, because a location needs different details from a weapon.

Some fields a Storyteller fills in later - availability lists, security traits and similar - so you won't see them here. Leave anything you don't know blank; a Storyteller can finish it when they approve.

## Writing a good one

The description is the part that gets read. Say what it looks like, where it came from, and what it does in play. A Storyteller deciding on twelve proposals in an evening will approve the one they can picture.

Check [Items & Locations](world-objects.md) first if you can - if your chronicle already has something close, you'll be asked to use that instead.

## Editing it afterwards

You can't, yet. Once an item is approved it belongs to the chronicle's catalog, and only a Storyteller edits that. Ask a Storyteller to change it, or propose a new one and ask them to retire the old.
