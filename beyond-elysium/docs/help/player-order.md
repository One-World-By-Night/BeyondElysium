# Your Own Order

Putting Rituals and Blood Magic paths in whatever order you like, instead of the chronicle's usual alphabetical or catalog order.

## Who can use this

Anyone editing a character sees this - a player editing their own character, or a Storyteller editing any character in the chronicle. Only these two sections offer it today: Rituals (in [Trait Lists](trait-editor.md)) and Blood Magic (in [Powers](power-editor.md)).

## How to get there

My Chronicle → Edit tab → Rituals or Blood Magic → **Reorder**.

## The screen

- **Reorder** replaces the section's usual Add/edit controls with a plain list of everything you hold there, in whatever order it's currently in.
- Each row - **▲** and **▼** buttons to move it up or down one place; on a computer, a drag handle (**⠿**) to drag it to a new spot directly.
- **Save order** - writes the new order immediately. **Cancel** - closes Reorder without saving.

## Common tasks

### Put your rituals or paths in your own order

1. Open the section and click **Reorder**.
2. Use **▲** / **▼** on each row (or, on a computer, drag it by **⠿**) until it's in the order you want.
3. Click **Save order**.

## Things to know

- **Saving takes effect right away** - it isn't a pending change, doesn't need a Storyteller's approval, and costs no XP. Only the order changes; nothing about what you hold is added, removed, or altered.
- **Drag handles are a computer-only shortcut.** On a phone or tablet, or from a keyboard, the ▲ / ▼ buttons do the same thing and are always there.
- **Adding or removing an entry doesn't lose your order** - a newly added ritual or path joins at the end; removing one leaves everything else exactly where it was.

## Troubleshooting

- **I don't see a Reorder button.** Only Rituals and Blood Magic support a custom order today - every other section still sorts itself. If you can't edit this character at all, see [Character Editor](character-editor.md).
- **"This list changed since you loaded it - reload and try again."** Someone else saved a change to this same list - another tab, or a Storyteller approving a change - after you opened Reorder. Reload the page and try again; nothing was saved partway.
- **Save order didn't do anything.** Check for the error message above the Save/Cancel buttons - a stale list (above) is the most common cause.
