# Storyteller Home

What waits on you in a chronicle, the next game night, who hasn't had attention, and the chronicle's numbers: your landing view in the Storyteller Toolkit.

## Who can use this

Anyone who has a tab in the Storyteller Toolkit: an HST, an AST, a Narrator or the chronicle's Harpy. Each part shows only to someone who can act on it. An HST or AST sees every part, the numbers included. A Narrator sees the downtime, plot, game night and Spotlight parts. Opening My Chronicle shows your own [Home](player-dashboard.md) instead, about your own characters.

## How to get there

- Storyteller Toolkit → **Home** (the first tab, where the page opens by default). On a phone it is **Home** in the bar at the bottom.

## The screen

- **Waiting on you** - one line for each thing that waits, left out when there is nothing. Each line opens the list it counts.
  - **Approval Queue** - "7 waiting · 2 XP requests · 1 Grapevine file · 1 new character". For an HST or AST. A new character counts when a member made it and it waits for you, either because the chronicle reviews new characters or because a pick needs a Storyteller; someone still asking to join is counted by their request instead.
  - **Downtime** - "3 to answer · 4 nobody has taken". The first number is yours and opens [My Queue](my-queue.md); the rest opens Downtime.
  - **Plot posts** - "2 waiting on you · 1 nobody has taken", counted the same way.
  - **Players** - "1 asks to join · 2 Discord links to approve". For an HST or AST. It opens [Players](chronicle-players.md).
  - **Your castings** - the NPCs you are cast as, with the night. It opens My Queue.
  - "Nothing waits on you." when there is nothing.
- **Next game night** - its date, time and place. Under it, how far downtime has come ("Downtime closes Fri, Nov 27, 23:59: 6 of 14 characters have sent actions") and how far the last night's reports have ("Reports for Sat, Nov 21: 3 of 9 have written theirs"), then **Open Downtime** or **Open Game Nights**.
- **Spotlight** - how many characters have had no staff post in the chronicle's spotlight days ("4 characters have had no staff post in 42 days") and the first five names. **Open Game Nights** has the full list; see [Game Nights](game-nights.md).
- **At a glance** - the chronicle's numbers, for an HST or AST. It is open on a computer and folded on a phone. It holds cards for **Characters** (by creature type), **Pending Changes**, **Active Plots**, **Characters Needing Attention**, **By Status** and **Players Without an Active Character** (click it to see who), and below them **Recent Activity**, the ten most recently submitted changes across the chronicle.

## Common tasks

### See what to do next

1. Open Storyteller Toolkit and pick a chronicle.
2. Read **Waiting on you** and click the line that matters.

### Check how downtime is coming along

1. Read **Next game night**. The numbers count active player characters that have sent at least one action for that night.

### See who has had no attention

1. Read **Spotlight**, then click **Open Game Nights** for the whole list.

### See which players have no active character

1. Open **At a glance** and click **Players Without an Active Character**.
2. Click the card again to collapse it.

## Things to know

- **The counts are kept for a minute.** Your own downtime and plot posts, and the Spotlight, can be up to a minute behind. The Approval Queue and Players lines are counted fresh each time.
- **"Nobody has taken" means no Storyteller is assigned.** A Storyteller picks one up by assigning it to themselves; see [My Queue](my-queue.md).
- **A Narrator sees no approvals and no players.** Those parts belong to the HST and AST.
- **Recent Activity isn't only reviewed changes.** A change still pending can show there too.
- **This reads the whole chronicle.** For one player's own view see [Home](player-dashboard.md); for one character's history see [Change History](sheet-history.md).

## Troubleshooting

- **"Failed to load your home."** Refresh and try again.
- **I don't see this tab.** You have no Storyteller role in the chronicle currently selected. Check the **Chronicle** dropdown, or ask an HST to check your role.
- **A number looks out of date.** Wait a minute or reload the page.
