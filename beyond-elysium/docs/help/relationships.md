# Relationships

A circular chart of character-to-character relationships in a chronicle, grouped by faction.

## Who can use this

Storytellers (HST and AST). A Narrator, the chronicle's Harpy, and a player never see this tab.

## How to get there

Storyteller Toolkit → Relationships.

## The screen

- **Focus on** - picks one character; the chart shows that character and everyone directly connected to them.
- **Show whole chronicle** - shows every connected character at once instead of one character's own circle. Only available while the chronicle has 60 or fewer connected characters; past that it stays unavailable so the chart doesn't become unreadable.
- **Faction** / **Creature type** - narrows the chart to one faction or one creature type at a time.
- The chart itself: one dot per character, arranged in a circle and colored by faction (characters with no faction are gray), with one line between any two characters who have a connection between them, whichever way the connection points and however many there are. The focused character's own dot is drawn larger. Names are written out in full, a long one wrapping onto a second line, and stay readable in dark mode. Click a name or its dot to open that character's sheet.
- **Connections** - under the chart, a plain list with one line for every pair drawn: the two names and the label, or how many connections they share. Click a line to open the same list of connections a line on the chart opens. It names everyone in full whatever the size of the screen.
- **A line** names the pair when you hover it and shows the connection's label, or how many connections it stands for. Click it, or tab to it and press Enter, to open a list of every connection between those two characters: who points at whom (each name opens that sheet), the label, the date it was made, and its notes with any Storyteller-only passages highlighted.

## Common tasks

### See who one character is connected to

1. Open Storyteller Toolkit → Relationships.
2. Pick the character under **Focus on**.

### See the whole web of relationships

1. Open Relationships.
2. Check **Show whole chronicle**. If it's unavailable, the chronicle has more than 60 connected characters - narrow with a filter first, or stay on one character's own circle.

### See what connects two characters

1. Open Relationships and find the line between them.
2. Click it. The list shows each connection between the two, with its label, date and notes.
3. Click a name in the list to open that character's sheet, or **Close** to go back to the chart.

### Narrow the chart to one faction

1. Open Relationships.
2. Pick the faction under **Faction**.

## Things to know

- **NPCs are on the chart like any other character**, so a player character's ties to an NPC show up, and an NPC can be the focus.
- **This chart only shows character-to-character connections** - a character's link to a plot, item, or location doesn't appear here. See [Connections](connections.md) for the general connection tool.
- **An unconnected character never appears here**, in any view - only characters who have at least one relationship with another character show up.
- **Two characters with several connections get one line**, not a stack of overlapping ones. Hover or open the line to see how many it stands for.
- **Faction color is just for grouping** - it carries no other meaning.

## Troubleshooting

- **"Failed to load relationships."** Refresh and try again.
- **"No characters match the current focus and filters."** Clear a filter, or pick a different focus character.
- **I don't see this tab at all.** You don't hold a Storyteller role in the chronicle currently selected - switch chronicles, or ask an HST/AST to check your role.
