# My Chronicle

The one page you use for everything in a chronicle you belong to: pick which chronicle,
then move between its Dashboard, Characters, Sheet, Edit, My Plots & Rumors, and Reports.

## Who can use this

Anyone who belongs to at least one chronicle - as a player, or as a Storyteller looking at
their own player-facing view here rather than the Storyteller Toolkit. If you don't belong
to any chronicle yet, the page has nothing to show below the chronicle picker.

## How to get there

Look for a "My Chronicle" link in your site's navigation - this plugin creates the page
automatically. If you can't find it, ask a Storyteller or your site admin where it's linked.

## The screen

- **Chronicle** - a dropdown listing every chronicle you belong to, each one labeled with
  your role there. Choosing a different one switches everything below it to that chronicle.
  If you don't belong to any chronicle, this says so instead of showing a dropdown, with a
  link to [send a Grapevine file to one](send-grapevine-file.md).
- **Tabs**, once a chronicle is selected:
  - **Dashboard** - your characters, your pending changes, and your plots at a glance. See
    [Dashboard](player-dashboard.md).
  - **Characters** - your characters here (every character, for a Storyteller of this
    chronicle), with **+ New Character** to start another. See [Characters](character-list.md).
  - **Sheet** - the selected character's sheet, or a prompt to pick one from Characters if
    none is selected yet. See [Character Sheet](character-sheet.md).
  - **Edit** - editing the selected character, or a blank form to start a new one if none is
    selected. See [Character Editor](character-editor.md).

Each tab shows what you can do in the chronicle selected - a Storyteller here sees the
Storyteller dashboard and controls; the same person in a chronicle where they only play sees
the player's.
  - **My Plots & Rumors** - plot threads you're connected to and any rumor that's reached
    you.
  - **Who's Who** - the chronicle's NPC directory, showing only the NPCs a Storyteller has
    made public and only what each one's profile shares. See [Who's Who](whos-who.md).
  - **What I Know** - secrets that have been revealed to any of your characters. See
    [What I Know](what-i-know.md).
  - **After-Game Report** - what your character did, what you want next, and anything for
    staff, one per character per session. See [After-Game Report](after-game-report.md).
  - **Reports** - a small picker among **Game Calendar**, **Location Cards**, and **Rote
    Cards**, open to every member, Storyteller or not. Game Calendar shows this chronicle's
    own honest note when it has no per-date schedule set up yet, rather than a blank page.
    Location Cards and Rote Cards each show one card per entry, with every field - including
    a Storyteller's own formatted Description or Appearance text - rendered the way it was
    written; a `[ST]...[/ST]` marked passage is still stripped out for you the same as
    everywhere else. A **Scope to character** picker above the tabs lets a mage pick which of
    their own characters to see Rote Cards for - the tab only appears once a character whose
    stack actually holds rotes is picked. See [Reports](reports.md).

The page keeps your chosen chronicle, tab, and character in its web address, so refreshing
or bookmarking it returns you to the same view.

## Common tasks

### Switch to a different chronicle

1. Open the **Chronicle** dropdown at the top of the page.
2. Pick the one you want. The tabs below reload for that chronicle.

### Jump straight to a tab

1. Click **Dashboard**, **Characters**, **Sheet**, **Edit**, **My Plots & Rumors**, or
   **Reports**.

### Look up a location's or rote's write-up

1. Open the **Reports** tab.
2. Pick **Location Cards** or **Rote Cards**.

## Things to know

- The **Chronicle** dropdown only lists chronicles you already belong to - it isn't a
  directory of every chronicle on the site.
- **Joining a new chronicle by hand-building a character isn't done from here.** A
  Storyteller shares a link to their chronicle's own character-creation page; starting a
  character there is your request to join. **Sending an existing Grapevine file is done from
  here** - see [Send a Grapevine File](send-grapevine-file.md), reachable from the
  Characters tab or the no-chronicle-yet message above. Either way, once a Storyteller
  approves it, that chronicle appears in this dropdown too.
- Switching the **Chronicle** dropdown while the Sheet or Edit tab has a character open
  doesn't clear that selection - the previously selected character belonged to the chronicle
  you just left. See Troubleshooting.

## Troubleshooting

- **"You don't belong to any chronicle yet."** Get a join link from a Storyteller and start
  a character there, or [send a Grapevine file](send-grapevine-file.md) if you already have
  one - see Things to know above.
- **"Your chronicles couldn't be loaded."** The request failed - click **Try again**.
- **A chronicle I just joined isn't in the dropdown.** Your first character there is likely
  still a pending join request - it appears once a Storyteller approves it.
- **I switched chronicles and my Sheet or Edit tab stopped showing the character I had
  open.** That character belongs to the chronicle you were just on. Go to **Characters** and
  pick one in the new chronicle instead.
- **Nothing loads at all.** Make sure you're logged in, then refresh the page.
