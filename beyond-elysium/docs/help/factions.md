# Factions

A sect, coterie, pack, chantry, court, or any other in-fiction group your chronicle tracks - who belongs, who leads, and what it's after. A faction has its own real "who can see this" setting, separate from its member list and its goals.

## Who can use this

Storytellers (HST and AST) create, edit, and delete factions directly, from the Storyteller Toolkit's **Factions** tab. A player never creates one this way - they propose one for their own character from **My Chronicle**, and a Storyteller approves it from the Approval Queue.

## How to get there

**Storyteller Toolkit → Factions**, or, for a player proposing one, **My Chronicle → Propose a Group**.

## The screen

- A list of every faction in the chronicle. Click one to open it.
- **New Faction** - name, type (a suggested list - Sect, Clan, Coterie, Pack, Chantry, Court, Cabal, Sept, Motley, House, Other - or type your own), an optional parent faction, a description, and its goals.
- Inside an opened faction: its description and goals, its own **Who can see this** (the same Everyone/Storytellers/rules choice a plot or item has), and its member roster.
- **Members** - each row shows a **leader** badge where it applies, a **Rank** text field (an internal title within the faction, like "Whip" - separate from a chronicle-wide [Position](positions.md)), a **Make leader** / **Demote** button, and **Remove**. Type a rank and click away to save it; the leader button saves immediately. **Add Member** picks from the chronicle's own active player characters.
- **Edit** and **Delete** on an opened faction. Deleting a faction removes its membership and un-links (never deletes) any positions scoped to it.

## Things to know

- **Goals are member-and-Storyteller-only, even when the faction itself is visible to everyone.** A plain viewer who can see a faction reads its name, type, and description; only a member of it, or a Storyteller, reads its goals - or its roster.
- **A leader can't remove themself or another leader.** Only a Storyteller can remove the last remaining leader, and only after promoting a replacement or deleting the faction outright - a faction always needs at least one leader while it has any members at all.
- **Only a Storyteller can promote or demote a leader.** Setting a member's rank is a smaller change - a leader can do that for their own faction too, from the same screen this document describes; there just isn't a menu path to it yet for a plain leader who isn't a Storyteller.
- **A player-proposed faction always goes to a Storyteller for approval**, even on a chronicle with auto-approve turned on for everything else - a faction is a chronicle-membership structure, not sheet data, and creating one is a real decision.
- **`[ST]...[/ST]` markers are stripped** from a faction's description and goals for anyone who isn't a Storyteller, the same as everywhere else.

## Troubleshooting

- **"Failed to load factions."** Refresh and try again.
- **"Failed to save this faction."** Make sure a name is filled in.
- **"Couldn't remove this member - a faction needs at least one leader."** Promote another member to leader first, or have a Storyteller remove this one.
- **"Couldn't update this member's leader status - a faction needs at least one leader."** Demoting this member would leave the faction with none - promote someone else first.
- **"Couldn't update this member's rank."** Refresh and try again.
- **A player's faction type was rejected.** A proposal is limited to Coterie, Pack, Cabal, Motley, or Other - a whole sect or court is a Storyteller's own call.
