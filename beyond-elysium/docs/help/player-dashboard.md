# Dashboard

Your personal landing view for a chronicle: your own characters, your own changes still awaiting review, and your own plots - never anyone else's.

## Who can use this

Any player in this chronicle. A Storyteller (HST or AST) opening this same tab sees a different, chronicle-wide dashboard instead - see [Game Dashboard](game-dashboard.md).

## How to get there

My Chronicle → Dashboard tab. It's the first tab, and where the page opens by default.

## The screen

- **My Characters** - every character you have in this chronicle, each name linking to its Sheet, with its creature type and status. "You have no characters in this chronicle yet." when there are none.
- **My Pending Changes** - changes you've submitted, across all your characters, that are still waiting on a Storyteller. Each line names the character, describes the change in plain language, and shows a badge for its approval level (`auto` or `st`). "Nothing pending review." when there's nothing waiting.
- **My Castings**, shown only when a Storyteller has cast you to play an NPC for an upcoming session - "You're playing {NPC} on {date}." Click it to open the [casting brief](npc-casting.md).
- **My Plots** - the same feed as the My Plots & Rumors tab: every plot thread you're connected to and any rumor that's reached you.

## Common tasks

### Check whether a change is still waiting

1. Open **My Chronicle** → **Dashboard**.
2. Look under **My Pending Changes**. If it's not listed there, it's already been decided - check that character's [Change History](sheet-history.md) for the outcome.

### Jump to a character's sheet

1. Under **My Characters**, click the character's name.

## Things to know

- Everything here is scoped to you - your characters, your changes, your plots. It never shows another player's data.
- A change drops off **My Pending Changes** the moment it's approved or rejected - it isn't a running log. For the full history, open that character's sheet and click **View history**.
- This view doesn't say why a change needs Storyteller review - only that it's still waiting.

## Troubleshooting

- **A change I submitted isn't pending here, but it's not on my sheet either.** It may have already been decided - check the character's [Change History](sheet-history.md) for whether it was approved or rejected.
- **A character I know is mine isn't listed.** It isn't linked to your account yet - ask a Storyteller to assign it to you.
- **"Failed to load your dashboard."** Refresh the page. If it keeps happening, tell a Storyteller.
