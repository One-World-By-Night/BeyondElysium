# Character Builder

A guided path for a new character. You work through your creature type's own steps, a bar at the top shows what each step costs as you go, and nothing is created until you press **Create character** on the last step. Stop whenever you like: the build is kept on your account, so you can pick it up on another device.

## Who can use this

Anyone who may start a character in the chronicle: a player, and someone who asked to join and is starting their first character. A Storyteller can open it too, but keeps the [Character Editor](character-editor.md) for NPCs, quick NPCs, sheets that already exist and extra sections.

## How to get there

My Chronicle → **+ New character**, on Home or on the Characters tab. Someone joining a chronicle reaches it from **Start a character** in the join panel. A Storyteller opens it from **Use the builder** on the editor's new character form.

## The screen

- **Steps.** A row of named steps on a computer, and one button that reads "Step 3 of 9 · Abilities" on a phone. Tap it to see the list. Every creature type has its own steps, read from its own sections and creation rules: Concept first, one step for each place the rules hand out points (Attributes, Abilities, Disciplines, Gifts and so on), a step for everything else, then Story and Review. Any step opens at any time, and nothing blocks **Next**.
- **Concept.** Name, creature type and the identity fields (Clan, Nature, Demeanor and the like). Identity comes first because the rules read it: your Clan decides which Disciplines are in-clan, your auspice decides your starting Rage.
- **The bar.** Pinned at the top. It shows the lines that belong to the step you are on, such as "Abilities: 3 of 5", the free points still unspent, and "XP 7 of 30 left". A line over its limit says where the rest comes from, first from the free points and then from XP. Tap the bar to open the whole tally.
- **Marks on the steps.** A step whose budgets are all used says **Done**. A step with something over its limit says **Over**.
- **Given by the rules.** Some values are handed to you: Rage by auspice, Gnosis by breed, Willpower, Conviction by creed, and entries such as a Wraith's first Status. They are filled in for you and listed on their step. If you change one yourself, the rules never write over it.
- **Warnings.** A pick that breaks a limit the book calls a guide, such as a Virtue above your creed's primary one, is flagged on its step and again on Review. It does not stop you.
- **Back and Next.** At the foot of every step. On a phone they stay in reach above the bottom bar.
- **Story.** Your character's background. It is optional, and you can add a portrait once the character exists.
- **Review.** Every pick, step by step, and then:
  - **The build.** What each budget used and what is left, and the XP against what a new character starts with.
  - **Must fix.** Things that stop **Create character**: no name, no creature type, a build that costs more XP than a new character starts with, or a rating a new character cannot start with.
  - **Warnings.** The guides above.
  - **Needs a Storyteller.** Each pick the chronicle's rules say a Storyteller must look at, with the reason.
  - **What happens next.** One line: it starts active, it starts pending, or it is a request to join.

## Common tasks

### Build a character across two devices

1. Start on your phone. The build saves a moment after each change; the foot of the screen says **Saving…** and then **Saved**.
2. Open **+ New character** on your computer. It offers **Continue** with your type, name and the day it was saved.
3. Press **Continue**. You are on the step you left.

**Start over** throws the saved build away after asking. There is one saved build per chronicle, and the last device to save wins.

### Create the character

1. Open **Review** and read **Must fix**. Clear anything listed.
2. Press **Create character**.
3. An active character opens on its Sheet. A pending one opens too, with **Waiting for a Storyteller** and the reasons. A request to join shows **Request Sent**.

### A pick that asks for a Coordinator

A pick the bylaws send to a Coordinator makes a new character start pending, as any pick the rules hold. On a chronicle that sends Coordinator approvals to the Archivist Toolkit, a Storyteller sends such picks from the pending character's **Build tally**, and the character becomes active once the Coordinator has approved each one. See [Coordinator Approval](coordinator-approval.md#a-new-character).

## Things to know

- **The server checks everything again.** Review is your own view of the build. When you press Create, the chronicle recounts it and trusts nothing from the saved build.
- **A pick a Storyteller must see makes the character start pending**, even in a chronicle where new characters otherwise start active. Review says which picks and why before you create it.
- **A build can never cost more than the starting XP.** The excess used to be silently free; it is refused now. A Storyteller entering a sheet that already exists ticks **Existing character** on the editor's form.
- **Your saved build is yours alone.** Storytellers never see it, and it is removed when you create the character, discard it or delete your account.

## Troubleshooting

- **Next is greyed out on Concept.** Pick a creature type first.
- **Create character is greyed out.** Review's **Must fix** lists what is missing.
- **The bar says nothing.** It waits for the first answer after you pick a creature type; give it a moment.
- **"This chronicle is not taking join requests right now."** You are not a member and the chronicle has closed requests, so there is nowhere to keep a build.
