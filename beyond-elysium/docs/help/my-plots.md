# My Plots & Rumors

Your own feed of ongoing stories: every plot one of your characters is connected to, every
rumor that's reached you, and the place you read what's happened and post your character's
actions.

## Who can use this

Any player - anyone with a character in the chronicle. What you see here is built from your
own characters: a plot they're directly connected to, or a rumor targeted at a group one of
them belongs to. If you also hold a Storyteller or Narrator role, that role's own Plots &
Rumors screen in the Storyteller Toolkit is separate from this one - this is your player
view. See [Plots & Rumors](plot-manager.md) for that screen.

## How to get there

My Chronicle → pick your chronicle → **My Plots & Rumors** tab.

## The screen

### The list

Every plot you're connected to, newest-updated first, as a titled button. Click one to open
it. If nothing has reached you yet, this says so instead of a list.

### A plot, once opened

- **← Back to your plots** - returns to the list.
- **Cover image**, if a Storyteller has set one.
- **Title**, then a row of details: a category badge if the plot has one, a status badge
  (the Storyteller's own status alongside what its dates say - pending, active, or finished),
  whether it's player-initiated or Storyteller-initiated, and a game date if it has one.
- **Overview** - the Storyteller's prose for what this plot is. Read-only here.
- **Under this plot** - any plots nested beneath this one, if there are any, each opening the
  same way when clicked.
- **Timeline** - every entry posted so far, oldest first: what type it is, its date, and its
  content. An entry created by a Storyteller's action-allocation tool or the background-use
  ledger renders as a short summary (what was spent, and the result) rather than raw text.
- A text box for posting your own entry, and **Post**.

## Common tasks

### Read what's happened in a plot

1. Open **My Plots & Rumors**.
2. Click the plot's title.
3. Read down the **Timeline**.

### Post an action

1. Open the plot.
2. Type what your character does into the box at the bottom of the Timeline.
3. Click **Post**.

### Follow a plot nested under another

1. Open the parent plot.
2. Under **Under this plot**, click the one you want.
3. Use **← Back to your plots** to return to your list when you're done, or open the parent
   again the same way.

## Things to know

- **This is a feed, not a directory.** It only ever shows plots connected to your own
  characters or rumors targeted at a group they belong to - it isn't a way to browse every
  plot in the chronicle.
- **You can only post an action.** A Storyteller's response, note, or resolution entries are
  theirs to write - you won't see a type picker here, only a plain box for your own action.
- **Some entries never show here at all.** A Storyteller's private notes on a plot are
  Storyteller-only and never reach this feed, even inside a plot you can otherwise read.
- **Each of your characters has its own plot**, named after the character. Only you and your
  chronicle's Storytellers see it, and each game date's actions for that character sit under
  it.
- **An action-allocation plot is yours alone.** If a Storyteller has turned one of your
  backgrounds into an action budget, only you (and a Storyteller) can see or post to that
  plot - it never appears for anyone else, even another player connected to the same story.
- **Posting adds to the story, it doesn't edit it.** There's no way to change or remove an
  entry once you've posted it - if you need to correct something, post again or ask a
  Storyteller.

## Troubleshooting

- **"Failed to load your plots."** Refresh and try again.
- **"Nothing yet."** No plot is connected to any of your characters, and no rumor has reached
  you - this fills in as your Storytellers connect you to a story.
- **"Failed to add this entry."** Try posting again. If it keeps failing, the plot may no
  longer exist, or it may not be yours to post to.
- **Post won't click.** The box is empty - type something first.
- **I know a plot exists but I can't find it here.** It isn't connected to any of your
  characters and no rumor has reached you for it - ask a Storyteller.
