# Coordinator Approval

The OWbN Character Bylaws say "Coordinator Approval" for a lot of things: a merit, a Discipline, a rating past the book's. Beyond Elysium can send such a change to the Archivist Toolkit for you, filed as an R&U Request in your own name, and apply it once the Coordinator approves it there.

It is a choice, not a gate. Nothing stops a Storyteller approving a change on their own, because the Coordinator's yes may already exist by email or in Discord.

## Who can use this

The HST and AST of a chronicle on the OWbN network, once the HST turns it on. A Narrator or a player doesn't see the buttons. A player sees where their change is.

## Turn it on

Chronicle Setup → **Coordinator approvals** → tick **Send Coordinator approvals to the Archivist Toolkit**. Only an HST can. It is off until someone does, and with it off nothing on this page happens.

The row tells you when the box can't be ticked yet:

- **This chronicle has no OWbN chronicle path.** The chronicle isn't linked to its OWbN record, so there's no chronicle to file under in the Toolkit.
- **This network has no Archivist Toolkit connection.** The site hasn't been connected to the Toolkit.
- **owbn-core is not running on this site.**

The demo chronicle can always turn it on. It shows what would be sent and sends nothing.

## What you see

A change needs a Coordinator when its reason says so. That reason comes from the chronicle's own rule (a rule whose reason reads **Coordinator Approval**) or, on a chronicle with the OWbN Character Bylaws switched on, from the bylaw clause that names the entry. In the [Approval Queue](approval-queue.md) the row says which Coordinator, and **Approve** asks:

- **Send to the Coordinator** files it in the Toolkit and keeps it waiting here.
- **Approve now** approves it yourself, with no Coordinator. Use it when the Coordinator has already said yes somewhere else. That's your call, and it is yours to answer for.

A change whose reason says **Coordinator Notify** asks differently: **Approve and send it in** approves it and files the R&U as the notification the bylaws ask for, or **Approve only**.

Any other waiting change has **Send to a Coordinator** beside **Edit**, for the time you know it needs one and the rules don't say so.

Approve Selected and a set's **Approve all** leave a Coordinator Approval alone and say how many they left, so a bulk approve never decides it for you.

## Send it

Pressing **Send to the Coordinator** opens a pop-up. It asks for:

1. **The character's record in the Archivist Toolkit.** Once for each character. The pop-up searches the Toolkit for the name, as you, and you pick the right one. If the character isn't there, register them in the Toolkit first; Beyond Elysium never files a Registration. **Change the link** redoes it.
2. **Regulation rules.** The Toolkit's own rules. The pop-up searches for the trait's name; tick the ones that apply, or search for others.
3. **Coordinators who must approve.** The offices to name. The ones the reason names are ticked already.
4. **A note to the Coordinator.**

With no rule and no Coordinator chosen, the Toolkit sends it to the character's clan or sect Coordinator.

The request is filed under your own account, the way it would be if you used the Toolkit's form: your email names you, and the Toolkit applies its own rules to you. You can only file for a character you could file for there. Next, your HST signs it at Staff Review (when you are the HST, that's you), then the Coordinator reviews it, then the Archivist. The Toolkit's own emails go out as for any R&U.

If the Toolkit doesn't answer, the row says **Sent, not confirmed**. Press **Send again**: it can't be filed twice. If the Toolkit refuses, you see its own message and nothing changes here.

Several changes sent together are filed as one list, so each approver gets one email.

## While it waits

The row says **With the Coordinator**, the request's number in the Toolkit (**R&U #33012**) and the step it is at. **Check now** reads it, and **Open in the Toolkit** opens it there. The change keeps holding its XP.

- The player can't withdraw it, and sees "With the Coordinator" in their History.
- You can't edit it, because the Toolkit has it as it was. You can **Approve now** or **Reject**. The request in the Toolkit stays open either way: answer or cancel it there.

Beyond Elysium reads the Toolkit every hour as the Storyteller who sent it. When the Coordinator has approved, it applies the change as that Storyteller, with the note "Approved by the Coordinator in the Archivist Toolkit (R&U #33012)", and takes the XP. When the request is denied, or cancelled in the Toolkit, the change is rejected with that note. A change a Storyteller already approved or rejected is left as it is.

If the Coordinator approved but the change can't be applied any more (the XP or the sheet moved), it stays waiting and the row says so. Approve it by hand.

## A new character

A pending character's picks that ask for a Coordinator are listed on its **Build tally**, under **Coordinator approval**, each with where its request stands. Send them from there. The character becomes active once the Coordinator has approved every pick that asks for approval. You can make it active yourself at any time, and a pick that was refused leaves it pending until you take the pick off or make it active anyway.

Picks that read Coordinator Notify are sent in as a notice and never hold the character.

## What it never does

It never edits, answers or cancels a request in the Toolkit after filing it, and it never uses an account other than yours. The connection's key stays on the server.
