# Players

Make an existing OWbN account a player in your chronicle, or take a player out. The HST and the AST both do this here. Staff roles (HST, AST, Narrator, Harpy) are set on [Chronicle Access](chronicle-access.md) by a site administrator. The tab only shows on a chronicle that is linked to OWbN roles.

## Who can use this

The chronicle's HST and AST, on a chronicle linked to OWbN roles. A Narrator, the Harpy and players don't see this tab, and neither does anyone on a chronicle that isn't linked.

## How to get there

Open the [Storyteller Toolkit](storyteller-toolkit.md), pick the chronicle, then choose **Players**.

## The screen

- **Add a player** - a search box. Type at least three letters of someone's name, or their exact email address. Each matching account shows with an **Add as player** button, or "Already a player". An email address only shows when you searched for that exact address.
- **Players** - everyone who is a player in this chronicle, with a **Remove** button beside each.
- A line under **Players** names the OWbN role that goes with being a player here, such as `chronicle/kony/player`.
- After you add or remove someone, a message says what happened, including what OWbN did.

## Common tasks

### Add a player

1. Type at least three letters of their name, or their exact email address.
2. Click **Add as player** beside the right account.
3. Read the message. "Is now a player here" means they can open the chronicle straight away.

### Remove a player

1. Click **Remove** beside them and confirm.
2. They are no longer a player here. Their characters stay exactly as they are.

## Things to know

- **Existing accounts only.** Someone with no OWbN account needs to sign in through OWbN once; after that you can find and add them.
- **The search covers every site on the network,** not only this one. When you add someone who has never visited this site, they are added to it too.
- **Staff are never changed here.** Adding an HST or AST as a player leaves their role alone, and **Remove** only takes out players.
- **OWbN roles follow.** Adding a player also grants them the chronicle's player role in OWbN, and removing them takes it away. If OWbN refuses, the message says why; the player is still added here, and an OWbN admin can grant the role.
- **Removing is not deleting.** Their account, their characters and their place in any other chronicle are untouched.

## Troubleshooting

- **There is no Players tab.** You are not the chronicle's HST or AST, or the chronicle isn't linked to OWbN roles. A site administrator links it by turning accessSchema on and giving the chronicle its role path on [Chronicle Access](chronicle-access.md). Until then, a site administrator adds its members there.
- **"No account matches."** Check the spelling, try their email address, or ask them to sign in through OWbN once.
- **"OWbN did not grant ..."** The player is added here. Pass the message to an OWbN admin, who can grant the role.
- **"... is on this chronicle's staff."** That account already holds a staff role here; nothing changed.
