# Draft a Plot from a Premise

A tool on Plots & Rumors that asks an AI to turn a one-line premise into a whole plot - its Storyteller-only beats and a couple of held rumors - in one step.

## Who can use this

Storytellers (HST and AST). A Narrator, the chronicle's Harpy, and a player never see this tool.

## How to get there

Storyteller Toolkit → Plots & Rumors → **Draft from a premise**.

## The screen

- **Premise** - a short line describing what's about to happen.
- **Characters and NPCs (optional)** - check off any you want the draft grounded in. Only each one's own public profile and identity fields are sent - nothing Storyteller-only from another character.
- **Factions (optional)** - check off any factions the draft should involve.
- **Draft and create** - the only button. There is no preview step: clicking it drafts the plot and creates it immediately, Storyteller-only beats, held rumors, and all, then opens the new plot.

## Common tasks

### Draft a plot from scratch

1. Open Storyteller Toolkit → Plots & Rumors.
2. Click **Draft from a premise**.
3. Type a one-line premise.
4. Click **Draft and create**.
5. You land on the new plot - review and edit it like any other.

### Ground a draft in specific characters or factions

1. Open the tool and type a premise.
2. Check off the characters, NPCs, or factions it should involve.
3. Click **Draft and create**.

## Things to know

- **There is no preview.** Unlike drafting an NPC's roleplaying notes or a session recap, this tool writes the plot the moment you click **Draft and create** - review happens on the plot itself afterward, not before.
- **Your chronicle has to turn on AI Assist first.** See [AI Writing Assist](writing-assist.md) and the [Admin Guide](../admin-guide.md#ai-writing-assist).
- **The new plot is Storyteller-only to start.** Its beats are posted as Storyteller-only notes; widen its audience afterward the normal way if you want players to see it.
- **Held rumors wait for a release** like any other rumor - see [Releases](release-batches.md).

## Troubleshooting

- **"AI assist is not configured yet..."** Nobody's set up a key yet, or your chronicle hasn't turned the feature on - ask an HST, AST, or your site admin.
- **"The AI provider replied, but not with the expected structure. Try again."** Click **Draft and create** again.
- **I don't see this button.** You don't hold a Storyteller role in this chronicle, or AI Assist isn't configured.
