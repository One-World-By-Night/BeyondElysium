# Home

Your landing view for a chronicle: your characters and what each has to spend, what needs you today, the next game night and what's new in your plots. It is never about anyone else.

## Who can use this

Anyone in the chronicle. A Storyteller opening My Chronicle sees their own Home, about their own characters. The Storyteller Toolkit has its own Home - see [Storyteller Home](game-dashboard.md).

## How to get there

My Chronicle → **Home** tab. It's the first tab, and where the page opens by default. On a phone it is **Home** in the bar at the bottom.

## The screen

- **Your characters** - a card for each player character you have in this chronicle (NPCs aren't listed). A card shows a portrait or initials, the character's name, its creature type, and a line such as "9 XP to spend · 1 XP held · 3 changes waiting". "In your cart" appears when this device holds a cart you haven't sent for that character. The name opens the Character section on the view you used last. **+ New character** and **Send a Grapevine file** sit under the cards. "You have no characters in this chronicle yet." when there are none.
- **Needs you** - what has a deadline comes first, soonest first, then what's new:
  - **Downtime** that is open while a character of yours has sent no actions, such as "Downtime for Sat, Nov 28 is open until Fri, Nov 27, 23:59." with the character's name under it. It opens that plot.
  - An **After-Game Report** you haven't written for a night your character attended, until its due date. It opens the report on that night and character.
  - Your newest unread **notices**, the same ones the bell holds. Opening one marks it read. "3 more unread in the bell" says how many are left over.
  - "Nothing needs you right now." when there is nothing.
- **Next game night** - its date, time and place, when downtime closes, and "You're playing {NPC}" for each casting you have that night.
- **Latest in your plots** - your five most recently updated plots and rumors, each with how long ago it changed, and **See all**.
- **Your groups** - the factions and positions your characters hold, when there are any.
- **Other chronicles** - a line for each other chronicle where you have changes waiting, such as "Boston: 2 changes waiting".

## Common tasks

### Check whether a change is still waiting

1. Open **Home**. A card's line says how many changes are waiting and how much XP they hold.
2. To see the changes themselves, open the character and click **History**; see [Change History](sheet-history.md).

### See what needs you before game night

1. Open **Home** and read **Needs you** from the top.

### Jump to a character

1. Click its name on its card.

## Things to know

- Everything here is yours: your characters, your deadlines, your plots. It never shows another player's data.
- A card's XP line says what you can spend now. A change waiting for a Storyteller holds its XP until it is decided; see [Character Editor](character-editor.md).
- Downtime counts as due until a character has sent at least one action for that night. Its deadline is the game night's own, or the one a Storyteller gave that character.
- A reminder goes out once, the day before downtime closes, as a notice and an email (the email only if you haven't turned off the chronicle's email).
- The list of every change you've made across chronicles is on each character's **History** now.

## Troubleshooting

- **A character I know is mine isn't on a card.** It isn't linked to your account yet, or it is an NPC. Ask a Storyteller.
- **Downtime is open but Needs you doesn't say so.** The window may have closed for your character, or an action has already been sent for that night.
- **"Failed to load your home."** Refresh the page. If it keeps happening, tell a Storyteller.
