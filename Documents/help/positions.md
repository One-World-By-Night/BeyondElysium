# Positions

A chronicle-wide office - Prince, Sheriff, Archbishop, Grand Elder, Duke, and the like -
optionally tied to a faction (a court's own Seneschal, say) or standing on its own. A position
keeps a full history of who has held it and when, not just who holds it now.

## Who can use this

Storytellers (HST and AST) create, edit, and delete positions, from the Storyteller Toolkit's
**Factions → Positions** tab.

## How to get there

**Storyteller Toolkit → Factions → Positions**.

## The screen

- A list of every position in the chronicle, each showing who currently holds it (or
  "Vacant"). Click one to open it.
- **New Position** - a title (pick from the preset list for the chronicle's own creature
  types, or type your own), an optional faction to scope it to, a holder, and whether the
  holder is publicly known.
- Inside an opened position: who holds it, its notes, and its full **History** - every past
  holder with when they started and, if they've since left, when.
- **Edit** and **Delete**. Deleting a position removes its history with it.

## Things to know

- **Changing the holder writes history automatically.** The outgoing holder's own row gets an
  end date, and a new row opens for whoever replaces them - or, if you vacate the position
  outright, the outgoing row simply closes with no replacement.
- **"Publicly known" hides who, not that.** With it unchecked, a non-Storyteller still sees the
  position is held - just not by whom, shown as "Held - not public." Uncheck it for an office
  whose current holder is a chronicle secret.
- **The preset list is a convenience, not a limit.** Every title is free text underneath - the
  preset groups (Camarilla court, Sabbat, Anarch, Garou sept, Changeling freehold, Mage
  chantry, Wraith necropolis) just save typing for the common ones.
- **The Query Tool can match a "Position" or "Group" condition regardless of who can see it** -
  a Storyteller building a query always sees the real value, even for a position marked not
  public or a faction marked Storytellers-only.

## Troubleshooting

- **"Failed to load positions."** Refresh and try again.
- **"Failed to save this position."** Make sure a title is filled in.
