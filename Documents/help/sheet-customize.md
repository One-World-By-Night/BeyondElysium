# Customize Appearance

Your own look for one character's sheet: font, colors, a background image, and a small graphic
behind each section. Purely visual - it never changes what the character can do.

## Who can use this

Storytellers (HST and AST) always have this. A player needs it turned on first - a site admin
does this from the WordPress profile screen, under "Sheet Customization." See
[Profile Settings](profile-settings.md).

Even with it turned on, you can only style a sheet you can already edit: your own character,
unless you're a Storyteller, who can style any character's sheet in the chronicle.

## How to get there

Open the character's Sheet, pick **Customize appearance** in the actions list, and click
**Go**. The list only offers it once both conditions above are met.

## The screen

- **Font** - a dropdown of preset fonts, or Default.
- **Accent color** / **Background color** / **Text color** - a color picker each.
- **Background image** - **Choose…** (or **Change…**, once one is set) opens your site's media
  library; the chosen image previews right below.
- **Section graphics** - one row per section on the sheet, each with its own **Choose…** /
  **Change…** button for a small image behind that section.
- **Reset to default** - clears every one of the above at once, immediately, with no
  confirmation.

Every control here saves the moment you change it - there's no separate Save button.

## Common tasks

### Change your sheet's font or colors

1. Open the character's Sheet, pick **Customize appearance**, and click **Go**.
2. Pick a font, or a color from any of the three pickers - each saves immediately.

### Set a background image

1. Open **Customize appearance**.
2. Click **Choose…** under Background image.
3. Pick or upload an image in the media library.

### Add a graphic behind one section

1. Open **Customize appearance**.
2. Find that section under Section graphics.
3. Click its **Choose…** button and pick an image.

### Remove all customization

1. Open **Customize appearance**.
2. Click **Reset to default**.

## Things to know

- **This is visual only.** Nothing here touches traits, powers, or XP - it changes how the
  sheet looks, never what it says.
- **Reset to default is immediate.** There's no confirmation - it clears the font, every color,
  the background image, and every section graphic in one click.
- **The grant is per user, not per character.** Once a site admin turns it on for your account,
  you can style every character you can already edit - it isn't switched on separately for each
  one.
- **A Storyteller with this grant can style any character's sheet in the chronicle** - a player
  with it can only style their own.

## Troubleshooting

- **The list doesn't offer Customize appearance.** Either you're looking at a character you can't edit, or
  you haven't been granted sheet customization yet - ask a site admin.
- **"Failed to load the current sheet style."** Refresh the page.
- **My change didn't seem to save.** Check for an error message near the top of the panel;
  reopen the panel to confirm what's actually saved.
