# What I Know

Every secret revealed to one of your characters, across every plot, item, location, or NPC it's attached to - one place, instead of hunting through each one separately. Also where you log what a character learned, for a Storyteller to turn into a real secret, and where you tell another character something you already know.

## Who can use this

Any player. Each secret shown is one your own character has actually been told, in character - this is never a way to see what you haven't learned.

## How to get there

My Chronicle → What I Know tab.

## The screen

**Log something I learned** - a folded form for recording what a character picked up: which of your characters, a title and the details, how (in game, downtime, rumor, told by someone, or other), and optionally who told them: pick from the list, which holds everyone in your Who's Who (your own characters in it included) plus any character a Storyteller has connected one of your characters to, or type a name - the box suggests names from that list as you type, and a name you type that is on it counts as that character. The character doing the learning is left off its own list. It goes to a Storyteller, who ties it to an existing secret or makes a new one from it - this is a request, not a secret itself, so you won't see its content until it's approved.

**Waiting** - any log or pass you've sent that hasn't been reviewed yet, or that a Storyteller refused (with their note, if they left one).

One card per secret you know, each showing:

- Its own title and write-up.
- What it's about - the plot, item, location, or NPC's name, when you can otherwise see that entity. If you can't (it's been kept from you some other way), the secret still shows, just without naming what it's about - learning a secret never quietly reveals a hidden entity's existence on its own.
- **Told by** the character who passed it to you, if that's how you learned it.
- When you learned it.
- **Tell someone** - if your chronicle allows it and the secret isn't already visible to everyone, pick a recipient from your Who's Who (any character you can see there, including an NPC) and send an optional note. Depending on how your chronicle has this set up, either the recipient reads it right away, or it waits for a Storyteller first - either way, the queue shows what's still waiting.

"You don't know any secrets yet." when there are none.

## Things to know

- **A secret you've been told stays here once revealed.** This is a record of what you've learned, not a live filter that can take something back.
- **Passing a secret always goes through a review step somewhere** - either before the recipient ever sees it, or afterward, depending on your chronicle's own setting. It's never instant and unreviewed.
- **You can't write a secret directly.** A log is a request - a Storyteller decides what it actually becomes and when.
- A Storyteller can also hold a reveal for a scheduled release - if that's happened, the secret simply isn't here yet until it goes out.
- Some chronicles turn this off entirely; if yours has, neither the log form nor "Tell someone" appears.

## Troubleshooting

- **"Failed to load what you know."** Refresh and try again.
- **I was told something in a scene but don't see it here.** A Storyteller records a reveal separately from playing it out in a scene - ask them to record it if it's missing, or log it yourself from this screen.
- **"Tell someone" isn't there.** Either your chronicle has logging and passing turned off, or the secret is already visible to everyone, so there's nothing to tell.
