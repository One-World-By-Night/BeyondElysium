# What I Know

Every secret a Storyteller has revealed to one of your characters, across every plot, item,
location, or NPC it's attached to - one place, instead of hunting through each one separately.

## Who can use this

Any player. Each secret shown is one your own character has actually been told, in character
- this is never a way to see what you haven't learned.

## How to get there

My Chronicle → What I Know tab.

## The screen

One card per secret you know, each showing:

- Its own title and write-up.
- What it's about - the plot, item, location, or NPC's name, when you can otherwise see that
  entity. If you can't (it's been kept from you some other way), the secret still shows, just
  without naming what it's about - learning a secret never quietly reveals a hidden entity's
  existence on its own.
- When you learned it.

"You don't know any secrets yet." when there are none.

## Things to know

- **A secret you were told can still disappear if a Storyteller changes its rules later** -
  this tab always reflects what you currently know, not a permanent record of everything
  you've ever been told.
- **This is not player-to-player secret sharing.** Every secret here came from a Storyteller
  choosing to tell your character something - there's no way for one player to pass a secret
  to another through this screen.
- A Storyteller can also hold a reveal for a scheduled release - if that's happened, the
  secret simply isn't here yet until it goes out.

## Troubleshooting

- **"Failed to load what you know."** Refresh and try again.
- **I was told something in a scene but don't see it here.** A Storyteller records a reveal
  separately from playing it out in a scene - ask them to record it if it's missing.
