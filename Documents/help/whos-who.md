# Who's Who

A directory of the chronicle's public profiles - NPCs and player characters alike - showing only what each one has chosen (or a Storyteller has chosen for an NPC) to make public: a display name, a description, a portrait, and for a player character, optionally who plays it. Never a sheet, an unpublished profile, or anything Storyteller-only.

## Who can use this

Any member of the chronicle. A player only sees a profile whose audience setting reaches them; a Storyteller (HST, AST, or Narrator) sees every profile in the chronicle regardless of that setting.

## How to get there

My Chronicle → Who's Who tab.

## The screen

Two groups, each a grid of cards - **Characters** (player characters with a public profile) and **NPCs**:

- **Portrait** - the character's own uploaded portrait if one is set, otherwise its Who's Who image (an NPC's own, or a character's main sheet portrait).
- **Name** - the Who's Who display name if a Storyteller set one (NPCs only), otherwise the character's real name.
- **Played by** - a player character's own account name, shown only when that player has ticked "Name me as the player behind this character."
- **Description** - the write-up for players to read. A `[ST]...[/ST]` marked passage is stripped out here the same as everywhere else.
- **Titles** / **Factions** - any public court position or faction membership the character holds, each filtered by its own visibility rule. A membership a Storyteller has marked hidden from profiles is left off for everyone but Storytellers.

A group with nothing in it is left off the page entirely, rather than shown empty.

Nothing here is editable from this screen. A Storyteller sets up an NPC's profile from its own [Character Editor](character-editor.md), in the Who's Who Profile section shown only to staff; a player sets up their own character's profile from the same editor, in a narrower version of that section shown only to them.

## Things to know

- **A character with no profile set up doesn't appear here at all**, for anyone but a Storyteller. Every character starts on the storytellers-only default - showing up in Who's Who is an opt-in step, not something that happens by creating or playing the character.
- **A player writes their own character's display name and description, and chooses whether it shows and whether their own name shows with it.** A Storyteller writes an NPC's, and alone can limit a profile to characters matching a rule.
- **This is not the character list or the roster.** A character's real sheet, assignment, XP, and Storyteller-only notes are never part of this projection.

## Troubleshooting

- **"Failed to load Who's Who."** Refresh and try again.
- **A character I expected to see isn't here.** Either its profile has never been shown, or its profile's audience doesn't currently reach you - ask a Storyteller, or check your own character's profile setting in the Character Editor.
