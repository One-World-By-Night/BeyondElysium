# My Chronicle

The one page you use for everything in a chronicle you belong to: pick which chronicle, then move between its Dashboard, Characters, Sheet, Edit, My Plots & Rumors, and Reports.

## Who can use this

Anyone signed in. Belonging to at least one chronicle - as a player, or as a Storyteller looking at their own player-facing view here rather than the Storyteller Toolkit - shows the full page below the chronicle picker; belonging to none still shows **Chronicles you can ask to join**.

## How to get there

Look for a "My Chronicle" link in your site's navigation - this plugin creates the page automatically. If you can't find it, ask a Storyteller or your site admin where it's linked.

## The screen

- **Chronicle** - a dropdown listing every chronicle you belong to, each one labeled with your role there. Choosing a different one switches everything below it to that chronicle. Any chronicle other than the one currently selected also shows how many of your own changes are still pending there, e.g. "- 2 pending" - a quick way to notice there's something waiting in a chronicle you aren't currently looking at. If you don't belong to any chronicle, this says so instead of showing a dropdown, with a link to [send a Grapevine file to one](send-grapevine-file.md).
- **Chronicles you can ask to join** - folded once you belong to a chronicle, open by default when you don't. Every chronicle on the site taking join requests that you aren't already a member of, each with an **Ask to join** button that opens the Join panel. See [Joining a Chronicle](joining.md).
- **Tabs**, once a chronicle is selected:
  - **Dashboard** - your characters, your changes - across every chronicle you belong to,
    not just this one - and your plots at a glance. See [Dashboard](player-dashboard.md).
  - **Characters** - your characters here (every character, for a Storyteller of this
    chronicle), with **+ New Character** to start another. See [Characters](character-list.md).
  - **Sheet** - the selected character's sheet, or a prompt to pick one from Characters if
    none is selected yet. See [Character Sheet](character-sheet.md).
  - **Edit** - editing the selected character, or a blank form to start a new one if none is
    selected. See [Character Editor](character-editor.md).

Each tab shows what you can do in the chronicle selected - a Storyteller here sees the Storyteller dashboard and controls; the same person in a chronicle where they only play sees the player's.
  - **My Plots & Rumors** - plot threads you're connected to and any rumor that's reached
    you.
  - **Who's Who** - the chronicle's public-profile directory, NPCs and player characters
    alike, showing only what each one's own profile shares. See [Who's Who](whos-who.md).
  - **What I Know** - secrets that have been revealed to any of your characters. See
    [What I Know](what-i-know.md).
  - **After-Game Report** - what your character did, what you want next, and anything for
    staff, one per character per session. See [After-Game Report](after-game-report.md).
  - **Reports** - a small picker among **Game Calendar**, **Location Cards**, and **Rote
    Cards**, open to every member, Storyteller or not. Game Calendar lists this chronicle's game nights, or says it has none.
    Location Cards and Rote Cards each show one card per entry, with every field - including
    a Storyteller's own formatted Description or Appearance text - rendered the way it was
    written; a `[ST]...[/ST]` marked passage is still stripped out for you the same as
    everywhere else. A **Scope to character** picker above the tabs lets a mage pick which of
    their own characters to see Rote Cards for - the tab only appears once a character whose
    stack actually holds rotes is picked. See [Reports](reports.md).

The page keeps your chosen chronicle, tab, and character in its web address, so refreshing or bookmarking it returns you to the same view.

## Common tasks

### Switch to a different chronicle

1. Open the **Chronicle** dropdown at the top of the page.
2. Pick the one you want. The tabs below reload for that chronicle.

### Jump straight to a tab

1. Click **Dashboard**, **Characters**, **Sheet**, **Edit**, **My Plots & Rumors**, or **Reports**.

### Look up a location's or rote's write-up

1. Open the **Reports** tab.
2. Pick **Location Cards** or **Rote Cards**.

### Ask to join a chronicle

1. Open **Chronicles you can ask to join**, or follow a join link a Storyteller shared with you.
2. Click **Ask to join** beside the one you want.
3. Write a short message and click **Send request** - or skip the message's own send and click **Start a character** or **Send a Grapevine file** instead, either of which is its own way to ask. See [Joining a Chronicle](joining.md).

## Things to know

- The **Chronicle** dropdown only lists chronicles you already belong to - it isn't a directory of every chronicle on the site. **Chronicles you can ask to join** is that directory, narrowed to the ones still taking requests.
- Switching the **Chronicle** dropdown while the Sheet or Edit tab has a character open doesn't clear that selection - the previously selected character belonged to the chronicle you just left. See Troubleshooting.

## Troubleshooting

- **"You don't belong to any chronicle yet."** Ask to join one from the list below it, or [send a Grapevine file](send-grapevine-file.md) if you already have one - see [Joining a Chronicle](joining.md).
- **"Your chronicles couldn't be loaded."** The request failed - click **Try again**.
- **A chronicle I just joined isn't in the dropdown.** Your join request, or the character you started for it, is likely still waiting on a Storyteller - it appears once they approve it.
- **I switched chronicles and my Sheet or Edit tab stopped showing the character I had open.** That character belongs to the chronicle you were just on. Go to **Characters** and pick one in the new chronicle instead.
- **Nothing loads at all.** Make sure you're logged in, then refresh the page.
