# Compendium

Your chronicle's catalog, one creature type and one section at a time: every Discipline, Gift, Merit, Ritual and the rest your chronicle has turned on, with what it costs, the book it comes from and any note your Storytellers wrote about it. It never shows book text.

## Who can use this

Anyone in the chronicle. A Storyteller also sees a creature type only Storytellers use, and a section the chronicle keeps closed to new picks.

## How to get there

My Chronicle → pick your chronicle → **Compendium** tab. From a trait's panel on the Sheet, Play or Edit, **See in the compendium** opens the same entry here.

## The screen

- **Creature type.** The types your chronicle has on. It opens on the type of your first active character.
- **Prices for.** Pick one of your characters and each row shows what the first step of that entry would cost them. A Storyteller can pick any player character. With no character chosen, a row shows the catalog's own cost.
- **Search this type.** Type two letters or more. It looks through the names in every section of the type, including the powers inside a Discipline or Gift, and lists what it finds with the section each one is in. Tap one to open it.
- **Has a chronicle note.** Shows only the entries your Storytellers have written a note on.
- **The section buttons.** One per section that holds entries, with how many it holds.
- **The list.** Entries are grouped the way the pickers group them. Each row shows the name, the price or cost, "Held x3" when your character has it, the book where the catalog names one, and a **Note** tag when the chronicle wrote one. The list shows 60 at a time; **Show more** adds the next 60.

Tap a row to open its panel: the chronicle's description and its source, the book, the levels of a power with what each costs, and what the entry needs. With a character chosen, the panel also says what the next step costs, whether it needs a Storyteller, and has **Add to cart**.

## Common tasks

### Look up what a Discipline costs

1. Open **Compendium** and pick the creature type.
2. Tap **Disciplines**.
3. Tap the Discipline. The panel lists each level and its cost.

### See what an entry would cost one of your characters

1. Pick the character under **Prices for**.
2. Find the entry. The row shows the price.
3. Tap it, then **Add to cart** if you want it. It goes into the same cart as Edit.

### Find an entry by name

1. Type part of its name into **Search this type**.
2. Tap the result. The section opens and so does the entry.

### Read only what your Storytellers have written about

1. Tick **Has a chronicle note**.

## Things to know

- **The book is the title the catalog records.** Many entries don't name one yet, and then the line is left out. Page numbers are not shown.
- **A note is the chronicle's own.** Storytellers write it; nothing here is copied out of a rulebook.
- **Prices are the editor's prices.** They come from the same place, so your chronicle's approval rules apply: a price can say a Storyteller has to approve it.
- **Merits and Flaws show the catalog cost, not a price.** Their picks are added in Edit, where the price is worked out.
- **Text a Storyteller marked as Storyteller-only never reaches you.** See [Storyteller-Only Content](storyteller-only.md).

## Troubleshooting

- **A creature type is missing.** The chronicle hasn't turned it on in Chronicle Setup, or only Storytellers use it.
- **A section is missing.** It holds no entries, or the chronicle closed it to new picks.
- **A row shows a cost and no price.** No character is chosen, or it is a Merit or a Flaw.
- **Add to cart isn't there.** The entry is one the panel can't add by a single step, or the character isn't yours.
