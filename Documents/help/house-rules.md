# House Rules

A live, read-only page listing every catalog item, power level, or power family your
chronicle has added a note to - its own reference material, description, or house rule -
grouped by which part of the catalog it belongs to.

## Who can use this

Any member of the chronicle - players and Storytellers alike see exactly the same page. You
need to be signed in and belong to the chronicle; there's nothing here that's hidden from a
player.

## How to get there

This isn't one of the fixed My Chronicle or Storyteller Toolkit tabs - a Storyteller or site
admin places it on a page of their choosing, using a widget or a shortcode. Ask where your
chronicle has linked it, or look for a "House Rules" link on your chronicle's own website.

## The screen

- One group per catalog section that has at least one note set, titled with that section's
  name.
- Inside each group, one entry per item, power level, or power family that carries a note,
  titled with its name, followed by whichever of these your chronicle has written for it:
  - **Reference** - a general note.
  - **Description** - the fuller explanation or house rule.
  - **Source** - where it comes from, or a citation.
- If nothing in the whole catalog has a note yet, the page says so instead of showing an
  empty list.

## Common tasks

### Look up a house rule for a specific item or power

1. Open the House Rules page.
2. Find the group for the section it belongs to (its catalog list's own name).
3. Read down to the item or power's own entry.

## Things to know

- **This page is read-only.** Nothing here can be changed from this screen - a Storyteller or
  admin writes these notes by editing the catalog item directly, elsewhere. See
  [Schema Blocks](schema-blocks.md).
- **Only items with something written show up.** An item with no Reference, Description, or
  Source set doesn't appear here at all, even if it exists in the catalog.
- **The same page can also print as a signed report.** A Storyteller running this from the
  Reports screen gets the identical content as a PDF; this page is the live, on-the-web
  version of the same list.
- **It updates as the catalog changes.** There's nothing to refresh by hand beyond reloading
  the page - whatever is currently written on the catalog is what shows here.

## Troubleshooting

- **"Could not load house rules."** Make sure you're signed in and a member of this
  chronicle, then refresh the page.
- **"No house rules are set on this catalog yet."** Nobody has written a Reference,
  Description, or Source on anything in the catalog yet - this isn't an error.
- **I can't find this page at all.** It isn't part of the fixed navigation - ask a
  Storyteller or your site admin where your chronicle has placed it.
