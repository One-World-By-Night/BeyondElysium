# NPC Casting Brief

What a chronicle member reads when a Storyteller casts them to play an NPC for one session:
the NPC's own name and public name, the session's date/time/place, its sheet's roleplaying
notes and every other non-Storyteller-only section, and any note the Storyteller wrote just
for this game.

## Who can use this

The member who was cast, from the moment they're cast until the end of the day after that
session's date - long enough to prep ahead, never open indefinitely. A Storyteller
(be_manage_characters) can always open it, to check what a cast player will see.

## How to get there

- [My Queue](my-queue.md)'s Castings section, if you're a Storyteller who's also been cast.
- Your Dashboard's **My Castings** card, on [My Chronicle](my-chronicle.md), if you're a
  plain player.

Either way, click the casting to open its brief.

## The screen

- The NPC's real name, and its public name if it has one set (see
  [Who's Who](whos-who.md)).
- The session's date, time, and place.
- Any note the Storyteller wrote for this specific game.
- The NPC's sheet, minus every Storyteller-only section except its roleplaying notes -
  Wants, Knows, Will Do If Unopposed, and the rest. You never see the NPC's XP, status,
  connections, secrets, or its real assigned player - only enough to play it.
- **Print** - the same content as a signed PDF, titled "Casting brief."

## Things to know

- **This is not the character sheet.** A cast NPC's assignment (who owns it long-term) and
  its Who's Who public profile are both separate things a Storyteller manages elsewhere -
  this screen is only ever the one-session loan of "how to play this tonight."
- **The window is generous on purpose.** It opens the moment you're cast - often days
  before the game, for prep - and stays open through the day after, not just during the
  session itself.
- **A Storyteller can cast anyone** - any member of the chronicle, not just other
  Storytellers.

## Troubleshooting

- **"You do not have permission to view this casting brief."** Either you weren't the one
  cast, or your access window has closed - ask a Storyteller.
- **"Failed to load this casting brief."** Refresh and try again.
