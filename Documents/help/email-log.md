# Email Log

Who Beyond Elysium emailed for this chronicle, what it was about, and whether the mail system took it. It also lists the emails the plugin decided not to send, with the reason.

## Who can use this

Storytellers (HST and AST), for their own chronicle. A Narrator, the chronicle's Harpy and a player never see this tab. Each chronicle has its own log: its Storytellers see its emails and nobody else's.

## How to get there

Storyteller Toolkit → **Email Log**. On a plot, **Who was emailed about this plot** opens it already narrowed to that plot.

## The screen

- **Search** - matches a name, an email address or a subject.
- **Kind**, **Result** and **Period** - narrow the list to one kind of email, one outcome, or the last day, week or month.
- The list, newest first, 20 to a page:
  - **When** - the date and time on the site's own clock.
  - **Recipient** - the person's name and the address the email went to, as they were at the time.
  - **What** - the kind of email, its subject, and the thing it was about when there is one. A plot's title opens that plot.
  - **Result** - **Sent**, **Not sent** with the reason, **Failed** with what the mail system said, or **In the daily digest**.
- When you arrive from a plot, a line above the list says "Only emails about" that plot, with **Show every email** to clear it.

## Common tasks

### Find out who got an email about a plot

1. Open the plot in [Plots & Rumors](plot-manager.md) and click **Who was emailed about this plot**.
2. Read the **Recipient** and **Result** columns. Everyone who was emailed shows **Sent**. Everyone who wasn't shows why.

### Find out why someone didn't get an email

1. Type their name or address into **Search**.
2. Look for **Not sent** in the **Result** column. The reason follows it.

## Things to know

- **A row says the mail system took the message. It can't say the message reached an inbox.** A message that lands in a spam folder or bounces after it left the plugin looks like any other **Sent**.
- **It never stores the body of an email.** A row holds who it was for, its subject line and what it was about. A subject can name a plot, so only Storytellers read the log.
- **Why an email isn't sent.** A person turned off email from Beyond Elysium on their own profile. The chronicle has email switched off under [Chronicle Access](chronicle-access.md). The person chose no plot emails, or a daily digest. The account has no email address. Or the chronicle is a demo, which never sends email.
- **The daily digest.** A person who chose a daily digest shows **In the daily digest** when the post happens. The digest itself, sent later, is its own row. A digest that covers two chronicles shows in the log of each.
- **What it covers.** Plot posts, things that became visible to a character, release batches, daily digests, change reviews, transfers, join requests and their answers, secrets told, sheets sent for review and their answers, and player invitations.
- **Rows are kept 90 days.** Older ones are deleted every day. Emails sent before the log existed aren't in it.
- **A failed send isn't retried.** The log shows it so you can tell the person.

## Troubleshooting

- **"No emails yet."** Nothing has been sent, or held back, for this chronicle in the last 90 days.
- **I don't see this tab.** You aren't an HST or AST in the chronicle currently selected. Switch chronicles, or ask an HST to check your role.
- **Someone says they got an email and it isn't listed.** The email may have come from another chronicle, from the site itself (a password reset, for instance) or from before the log existed.
