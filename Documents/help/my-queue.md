# My Queue

What's personally on your plate in this chronicle: unanswered downtime assigned to you,
ordinary plots waiting on your own reply, and what nobody has claimed yet.

## Who can use this

Anyone holding Plots & Rumors access or Character management access (HST, AST, Narrator).
A player never sees this screen.

## How to get there

Storyteller Toolkit → My Queue tab, right after Dashboard.

## The screen

- **Downtime** - unanswered action plots assigned to you, across every game date. Click a
  row to open that character's plot thread and write the answer.
- **Plots** - ordinary plots (not action plots) assigned to you whose newest player post is
  newer than your own newest reply - the ones actually waiting on you, not everything you
  happen to be listed as owning.
- **Castings** - NPCs you're yourself cast to play for a session today or later, if a
  Storyteller has cast you (anyone in the chronicle can be, not staff only). Click one to
  open its [casting brief](npc-casting.md).
- **Unassigned** - a count of unanswered downtime and unanswered plot posts nobody owns at
  all, so nothing quietly falls through with no owner.

## Assigning yourself or someone else

Assignment isn't set here - it's set where the plot or character already lives:

- On a plot: the plot editor's own assignee picker.
- On a downtime row: the picker on that row in the [Downtime](downtime-queue.md) queue.
- On an NPC: the assignee picker in the character editor, NPCs only.

Only a chronicle member holding HST, AST, or Narrator can be assigned anything.

## Things to know

- Assignment is advisory, not a permission gate - any Storyteller can still act on any
  plot or NPC regardless of who it's assigned to. It only changes what shows up here.
- A plot's own newest-post comparison only counts a real posted reply - a held answer,
  waiting on a release batch, does not count as your reply yet (see
  [Releases](release-batches.md)).

## Troubleshooting

- **"Failed to load My Queue."** Refresh and try again.
- **I don't see this tab at all.** You don't hold Plots & Rumors or Character management
  access in the chronicle currently selected.
- **A plot I'm assigned isn't showing up.** It only appears once a player has posted
  something newer than your own last reply - an assigned plot with nothing new to answer
  stays out of the list on purpose.
