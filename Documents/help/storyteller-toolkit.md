# Storyteller Toolkit

The Storyteller-facing hub for a chronicle: pick which chronicle, then move between its
Dashboard, Approval Queue, Plots & Rumors, and Boon Ledger.

## Who can use this

Storytellers (HST and AST) see all four tabs. A Narrator sees only Plots & Rumors. The
chronicle's Harpy sees only Boon Ledger. A plain player sees none of these tabs - their own
view of a chronicle is [My Chronicle](my-chronicle.md), not this page.

## How to get there

Look for a "Storyteller Toolkit" link in your site's navigation - this plugin creates the
page automatically. If you can't find it, ask a Storyteller or your site admin where it's
linked.

## The screen

- **Chronicle** - a dropdown listing every chronicle you belong to, each one labeled with
  your role there. Choosing a different one reloads the tabs below for your role in that
  chronicle specifically. If you don't belong to any chronicle, this says so instead of
  showing a dropdown.
- **Tabs**, each shown only when you hold the matching role in the chronicle currently
  selected:
  - **Dashboard** - chronicle-wide character, change, and plot stats. See
    [Game Dashboard](game-dashboard.md).
  - **Approval Queue** - every pending sheet change, in one list. See
        [Approval Queue](approval-queue.md).
  - **Players** - add an existing OWbN account as a player, or take a player out (HST and AST). See [Players](chronicle-players.md).
  - **Plots & Rumors** - create and run plots, allocate actions, generate rumors. See
    [Plots & Rumors](plot-manager.md).
  - **Boon Ledger** - the chronicle's Harpy view. See [Boon Ledger](boon-ledger.md).
- If none of the four apply to your role in the chosen chronicle, the page says "You don't
  hold a Storyteller role in this chronicle." instead of showing any tabs.

The page keeps your chosen chronicle and tab in its web address, so refreshing or
bookmarking it returns you to the same view.

## Common tasks

### Switch to a different chronicle

1. Open the **Chronicle** dropdown at the top of the page.
2. Pick the one you want. The tabs below reload for your role there.

### Jump straight to a tool

1. Click **Dashboard**, **Approval Queue**, **Plots & Rumors**, or **Boon Ledger** -
   whichever tabs are showing.

## Things to know

- **Tabs are chronicle-specific.** Switching to a chronicle where you hold a narrower role
  hides tabs you had a moment ago; the page falls back to the first tab still available to
  you there.
- **This is the Storyteller-facing page.** Your own characters and your own plot feed live
  on [My Chronicle](my-chronicle.md) instead, even if you're also an HST or AST - a
  Storyteller opening My Chronicle's own Dashboard tab sees the same chronicle-wide view
  described in [Game Dashboard](game-dashboard.md), but every other My Chronicle tab is that
  Storyteller's own player-facing view.
- **A Narrator's Plots & Rumors access here is the same as a Storyteller's** - it includes
  the Allocate actions and Generate rumors tools, not only posting responses and notes.
- **The chronicle's Harpy sees only Boon Ledger here** - not Characters, Plots & Rumors, or
  the Approval Queue, even though a Harpy can look up characters to see who owes whom.

## Troubleshooting

- **"You don't hold a Storyteller role in this chronicle."** You're a plain player in the
  chronicle currently selected, or you're in the wrong chronicle - check the **Chronicle**
  dropdown, or ask an HST/AST to check your role.
- **"You don't belong to any chronicle yet."** You have no membership anywhere yet - ask a
  Storyteller for a join link.
- **A tab I expect isn't showing.** You hold a narrower role in the chronicle currently
  selected than you thought - switch chronicles, or ask an HST/AST to check your role there.
- **Nothing loads at all.** Make sure you're logged in, then refresh the page.
